- In **October 2018**, Amazon raised its minimum wage to **\$15/hour** for all US employees — more than double the federal minimum of \$7.25/hour at the time
- Affected over **250,000** full-time Amazon employees and 100,000+ seasonal workers
- Widely seen as a mix of genuine employee welfare and a PR move to pressure competitors and Congress into doing the same
- Shortly after, Amazon began [[Lobbying|lobbying]] Congress to raise the federal minimum wage nationwide — arguing that if Amazon could do it, so could everyone else

*Interesting Fact:* The announcement came just weeks after Senator Bernie Sanders publicly attacked Amazon over workers relying on government benefits — and introduced a bill literally called the **"Stop BEZOS Act"**
