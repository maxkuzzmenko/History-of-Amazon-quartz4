- Stands for **[Department of Defense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Department_of_Defense)**
- The US federal department responsible for the military- Army, Navy, Air Force, Marines, Space Force, etc.
- One of the largest organisations in the world, with over 1.3 million active-duty personnel
- Also one of the biggest spenders on tech contracts- companies like Amazon ([AWS](AWS.md)), Microsoft, and Google compete heavily for DoD cloud deals

*Interesting Fact:* The DoD's headquarters is the **[Pentagon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Pentagon)**- the world's largest office building by floor area
