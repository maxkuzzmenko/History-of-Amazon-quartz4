- For its first **9 years**, Amazon made no profit- every penny earned was pumped back into growth
- Wall Street was sceptical for years, but Bezos consistently argued that long-term growth mattered more than short-term profits
- In **2003**, Amazon posted its first ever full-year profit- a net income of **\$35 million** on **\$5.3 billion** in revenue
- Modest by today's standards, but a massive turning point that proved the business model actually worked

*Interesting Fact:* Amazon's first ever profitable *quarter* actually came in **Q4 2001**- but it took another two years to turn a full year green
