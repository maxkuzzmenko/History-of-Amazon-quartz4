- Short for **Amazon's Second Headquarters**
- In **2017**, Amazon announced it was looking for a second headquarters city to complement its Seattle base
- Turned into a massive **public bidding war** — **238 cities and regions** across North America submitted pitches, offering billions in tax breaks to lure Amazon
- Amazon ultimately split HQ2 between two locations in **2018**:
	- **[Arlington, Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arlington,_Virginia)** (National Landing) — near Washington D.C.
	- **[Long Island City, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Island_City,_Queens)** — though Amazon later pulled out of New York due to public and political anger
- Virginia gave Amazon an estimated **$750 million** in tax breaks and cash deals

*Interesting Fact:* The whole process was widely criticised as a PR stunt — Amazon likely already knew where it wanted to go, and used the competition to squeeze as much free money out of desperate cities as possible
