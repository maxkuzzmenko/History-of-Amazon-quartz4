- On **September 4, 2018**, Amazon briefly hit a **$1 trillion market cap** — meaning the total value of all its shares combined reached $1 trillion
- Made Amazon only the **second US company ever** to reach this milestone, just days after [Apple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_Inc.) did it first
- At the time, Bezos was the richest person in the world
- To put it in perspective — $1 trillion is more than the entire GDP of most countries

*Interesting Fact:* Amazon's market cap was under **$500 billion** just two years earlier in 2016 — it doubled in value in under two years
