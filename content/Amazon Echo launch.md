- The **Amazon Echo** is a smart speaker with a built-in voice assistant called **Alexa**
- First released to [Amazon Prime](Amazon Prime.md) members in late **2014**, then to the general public in **2015**
- You could ask it questions, play music, set timers, control smart home devices, and shop on Amazon- all by just speaking
- Was widely mocked when announced- many couldn't see why anyone would want a always-listening speaker in their home
- Became a massive hit anyway, and kickstarted the entire smart speaker market- Google and Apple both rushed out competitors shortly after

*Interesting Fact:* Alexa was named after the ancient [Library of Alexandria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Library_of_Alexandria)- the team wanted a name that felt associated with knowledge
