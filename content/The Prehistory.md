- Original name was “Cadabra, Inc.”, but was misheard by his lawyer as “Cadaver, Inc.”
- He went through the dictionary, searching for a better name; Amazon seemed a good choice because it’s “exotic and different”, so he settled with “Amazon.com, Inc.”
- It was originally selling books 
- Was originally founded in Bezos’ garage, Bellevue, Washington![Photos: Amazon's humble beginnings out of Jeff Bezos' garage](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcTKYMHtwEW5P22zyXSjqXNdbPdC1qLq40oL7w&s)
 
- He chose Bellevue due to its its abundance of technical talent from [Microsoft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft) and the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington), as well as its smaller population for [sales tax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sales_tax) purposes and the proximity to a major book distribution warehouse in [Roseburg, Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roseburg,_Oregon)
- *Interesting Fact:* Bezos bought www.relentless.com, which he considered to be the name of the company; it now redirects to Amazon's homepage
