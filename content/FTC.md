- Stands for **[Federal Trade Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Trade_Commission)**
- A US government agency responsible for protecting consumers and making sure companies compete fairly
- Basically, they make sure big companies don't abuse their power to crush competition or deceive customers
- Has the authority to investigate, fine, and sue companies — including blocking two companies from merging into one, or forcing a company to be broken apart

*Interesting Fact:* The FTC was founded in **1914**, originally to break up trusts like Standard Oil's successor companies
