- An independent trade union formed entirely by Amazon warehouse workers themselves- not backed by any larger existing union
- Founded by **Chris Smalls**, a former Amazon employee who was fired in 2020 after organising a protest over poor COVID-19 safety conditions
- Won the historic union vote at Amazon's **JFK8 warehouse** in Staten Island in April 2022- the first successful Amazon union in US history
- Ran their campaign on a budget of roughly **$120,000**- a fraction of what Amazon spent trying to stop them

*Interesting Fact:* After Smalls was fired, an internal Amazon memo leaked calling him "not smart or articulate"- which backfired completely, drawing national media attention to him and the union effort
