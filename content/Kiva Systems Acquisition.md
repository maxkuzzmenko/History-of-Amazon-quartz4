- In **2012**, Amazon bought **[[Amazon Robotics|Kiva Systems]]** for **$775 million**
- Kiva made small orange robots that drove underneath shelving units and carried them to human workers- instead of workers walking miles around a warehouse to find items, the shelves came to them
- Amazon later rebranded the company as **Amazon Robotics**
- Completely changed how Amazon's warehouses operate, making order fulfilment dramatically faster and cheaper

*Interesting Fact:* Before Kiva robots, warehouse workers were walking up to **15 miles per shift** to pick and pack orders
