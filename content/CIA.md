- Stands for **[Central Intelligence Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Intelligence_Agency)**
- The US government's main spy agency — responsible for gathering and analysing information about foreign governments, companies, and individuals
- Reports directly to the President
- In **2013**, the CIA awarded [AWS](AWS.md) a **$600 million cloud computing contract**, marking a major move toward using private companies to store and manage classified government data

*Interesting Fact:* The CIA's exact budget is kept secret, but it's estimated to be around **$15 billion per year**
