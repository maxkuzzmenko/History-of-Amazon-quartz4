- Amazon's attempt at making a smartphone, launched in **June 2014**
- Its headline feature was **Dynamic Perspective**- a gimmicky 3D-like display effect that used front-facing cameras to track the user's face
- Also included **Firefly**, a feature that could identify real-world objects through the camera and let you buy them on Amazon instantly
- Priced at **\$199** on contract- quickly dropped to **99 cents** as sales tanked
- Discontinued just **13 weeks** after launch
- Amazon wrote off **\$170 million** in unsold stock

*Interesting Fact:* Bezos reportedly called the Fire Phone "an embarrassing failure"- it's now studied as a classic example of what not to do when launching a product
