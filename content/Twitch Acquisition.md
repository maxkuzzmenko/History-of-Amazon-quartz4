- **[Twitch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twitch_(service))** is a live streaming platform, mainly used for watching people play video games
- In **2014**, Google was reportedly in advanced talks to buy Twitch for around **$1 billion**
- Amazon swooped in and bought it instead for **$970 million** in cash
- At the time many people questioned why Amazon wanted a gaming platform- it's since grown into one of the most visited sites on the internet, with over **30 million daily visitors**

*Interesting Fact:* Twitch started in **2011** as a spin-off of a general life-streaming site called *[Justin.tv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin.tv)*, where one of the founders just streamed his own daily life 24/7
