###### General Info:
- Stands for **Amazon Web Services**, officially "Amazon Web Services, Inc."
- Provides on-demand [cloud computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cloud_computing) platforms and APIs to individuals, companies and governments
- Works on a **pay-as-you-go** basis- you only pay for what you actually use
- Started as an internal project at Amazon, it quickly became the most profitable part of the entire company
- Today the **world's largest cloud provider**- ahead of Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud

###### Origins:
- [[AWS Launch (Web Services)]]- 2002
- [[AWS Launch (API Mandate)]]- 2002
- [[AWS Launch (Cloud Computing)]]- 2006

*Interesting Fact:* AWS makes up only a small portion of Amazon's total revenue- but generates the **vast majority of its profits**. Without AWS, Amazon as a whole would earn significantly less
