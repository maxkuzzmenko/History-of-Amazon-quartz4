- A massive economic bubble that built up between roughly **1995-2000**, driven by investors blindly throwing money at internet-based companies
- Investors were pouring money into any company with ".com" in its name, regardless of whether it actually made money
- The bubble burst in **March 2000**, wiping out trillions of dollars and bankrupting hundreds of companies
- Amazon survived- but its stock dropped from **~\$107 to ~\$7** at its lowest point

*Interesting Fact:* Many companies that collapsed during the crash (e.g. Pets.com, Webvan) had spent millions on Super Bowl ads just months before going bankrupt
