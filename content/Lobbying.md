- The practice of hiring people (lobbyists) to directly influence politicians and the laws they write, on behalf of a company or group
- Completely legal in the US — lobbyists meet with lawmakers, push for favourable rules, and fight against regulations that could hurt their client
- Companies also donate to political campaigns through **PACs** (Political Action Committees) — basically a way to funnel money into politics legally
- The more a company spends, the more access and influence it tends to have in Washington

*Interesting Fact:* The term "lobbying" comes from the lobbies of government buildings, where people would wait to corner politicians and pitch their interests
