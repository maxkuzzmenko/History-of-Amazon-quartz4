- Launched on **February 2, 2005** for **$79/year**, offering unlimited free two-day shipping on eligible items
- Most analysts at the time thought it was a terrible idea — Amazon would lose money on every delivery
- It worked because it made customers feel they needed to buy *more* from Amazon to get their money's worth — and they did
- Over time it expanded far beyond shipping, adding **[Prime Video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Video)**, **[Prime Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_Music)**, and more
- Now has over **200 million subscribers** worldwide, making it one of the most successful subscription services ever

*Interesting Fact:* The name "Prime" was chosen internally in just a few days — the team nearly called it "Super Saver Platinum"
