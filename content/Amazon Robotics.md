- Amazon's robotics division, originally a separate company called Kiva Systems before Amazon bought it in 2012 for **$775 million**
- Makes the small orange robots seen in Amazon warehouses- they drive under shelving pods and carry the whole shelf to a human worker, instead of the worker walking miles to find items
- After the acquisition, Amazon rebranded Kiva Systems as **Amazon Robotics** and stopped selling the robots to other companies- keeping the technology exclusively for itself
- Has since deployed **hundreds of thousands** of robots across Amazon's warehouses worldwide

*Interesting Fact:* Before the robots, warehouse workers were walking up to **15 miles per shift** just to pick and pack orders
