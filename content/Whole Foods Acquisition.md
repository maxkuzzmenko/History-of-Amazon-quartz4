- In **June 2017**, Amazon bought **[Whole Foods Market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whole_Foods_Market)** for **$13.7 billion** — its biggest acquisition ever at the time
- Whole Foods is a high-end American supermarket chain, sometimes nicknamed **"Whole Paycheck"** due to its expensive prices
- The day the deal was announced, shares in every major US supermarket chain crashed — the industry saw it as a direct threat
- Amazon immediately started offering [[Amazon Prime launch|Prime]] member discounts in Whole Foods stores, tying the two together
- Marked Amazon's first serious push into physical shops and the grocery market

*Interesting Fact:* The deal was reportedly struck in just a few weeks after Bezos and Whole Foods CEO [John Mackey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Mackey_(businessman)) met in person — unusually fast for a deal of this size
